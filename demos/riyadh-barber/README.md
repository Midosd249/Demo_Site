# BRONZE / Barber Studio Demo

Second field-sales website for the Riyadh portfolio.

## Purpose

This repository is the **website upsell layer**, not the digital-menu product. BRONZE demonstrates a premium service-business website that can be sold independently or alongside the separate menu repository.

## Conversion model

- Strong brand-first hero.
- Services with starting price and duration.
- Service detail sheet.
- Booking path that prepares a WhatsApp message with service context.
- Space/gallery proof.
- Location and direct-call actions.
- Sticky mobile booking access.
- Arabic-first RTL with an English toggle.

## Demo policy

BRONZE is fictional DEMO content. It does not claim a real business, phone number, rating, address, staff member, availability, or performance result.

Before client delivery replace the brand, photography, service data, WhatsApp number, booking integration, location, opening hours, legal details, metadata, and structured data.

## Assets

Demo photography uses remote Unsplash URLs and must be replaced with client-owned or properly licensed assets before production.

## Sales sequence

1. Show the hero for five seconds.
2. Open Services.
3. Tap a service and show price + duration.
4. Choose the service and show the prepared WhatsApp booking.
5. Show the room/gallery.
6. Finish on the booking/location section.
7. Position the site as the business's owned conversion layer; the separate menu product can remain a separate system.
