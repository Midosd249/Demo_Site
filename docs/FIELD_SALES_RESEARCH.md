# FIELD SALES RESEARCH — Saudi/GCC Website Portfolio

## Purpose

This is the research ledger behind the field-sales website portfolio. It is not a generic SEO report. Its job is to inform which websites we build, how each sector is presented, what the owner should see during a phone demo, and what recurring website/growth service can follow a launch.

## High-signal conclusions

### 1. The website is a conversion instrument
The strongest recurring pattern in current Saudi-focused research is to design around what the visitor needs to understand and do: service clarity, trust, local context, and an immediate contact/booking/quote path.

### 2. Arabic/RTL is product design, not translation
Saudi bilingual sites need native RTL behavior, readable Arabic typography, correct directional controls, and language-specific content/URLs where appropriate.

### 3. Local facts are a trust layer
Business identity, location, hours, services, phone/contact and service areas must be accurate and consistent.

### 4. Structured data should expose truth, not marketing claims
Google's LocalBusiness documentation supports business details such as name, address, hours, telephone and URL and recommends the most specific applicable subtype. Google's general structured-data guidance says markup should describe visible page content and emphasizes complete, accurate properties over inaccurate stuffing.

### 5. AI/search readiness is extractability
The useful goal is to make the business easy for machines to understand: explicit business entity, services, location, hours, FAQs, contact routes, prices/ranges where appropriate, and consistent structured data.

### 6. Measure high-intent actions
Prioritize WhatsApp starts, calls, bookings, quote requests, forms and qualified leads. Impressions and traffic are supporting indicators.

## Riyadh field-sales portfolio rule

The portfolio should be intentionally mixed so that one route can encounter several buyer types without showing the same design twice.

Recommended first ten: Premium barber; ladies salon/spa; dental/aesthetics clinic; car detailing/ceramic; boutique fitness; fashion/abaya; home services; florist/gifts; events/weddings; professional services.

## Research quality rule

Official Google and Saudi statistical sources outrank agency blogs and vendor marketing. Vendor/agency sources are useful for observing local commercial patterns, not for proving universal claims. Market-size claims from commercial research should be treated as directional unless independently corroborated.

## What to research before each build

1. Saudi/Riyadh buyer behavior
2. Category leaders and strong local websites
3. Mobile/booking/contact patterns
4. Category-specific compliance
5. Search/structured-data requirements
6. Strong visual references
7. Permissively licensed/open assets
8. Recurring growth opportunities
9. Field-sales objections
10. The shortest convincing demo choreography

Then update this ledger with the strongest findings before or during implementation.

## Asset sourcing rule

GitHub repositories, public templates, design systems and open-source code are research/reference inputs, not automatic code donors.

Before reusing code/assets, verify license, attribution requirements, stack compatibility, security/dependency implications, generic reusability, and whether reuse would make multiple demos visually indistinguishable.

Never copy a competitor's proprietary design wholesale.
