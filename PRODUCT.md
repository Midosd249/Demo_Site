# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Saudi/GCC small-business owners and the people presenting websites to them during sales conversations. The end customer is a local service-business visitor who needs to understand the offer quickly and take a conversion action on mobile.

## Product Purpose

This repository is the website layer of a broader sales offering. It provides polished, sector-specific business websites that strengthen brand presentation, local visibility, service discovery, booking or enquiry conversion, and ongoing client growth. Digital-menu creation and QR-menu workflows are intentionally handled by a separate repository.

## Positioning

The website layer complements the separate digital-menu product instead of duplicating it: the menu solves food-service ordering/menu presentation, while this repository sells the broader business presence around brand, search, services, booking, enquiry, location, and conversion.

## Operating Context

The primary evaluation scene is a field sales visit in Riyadh where a prospect is shown a working demo on a phone or laptop. Each demo must communicate value within seconds and make the next action obvious.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include static website demos with responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Arabic-first RTL presentation, bilingual presentation where useful, WhatsApp/call/maps conversion paths, service/package presentation, galleries, booking/enquiry patterns, and mobile sticky actions. Demos use clearly marked fictional content and replaceable imagery. No digital-menu functionality belongs in this repository.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing website portfolio documentation and the BRONZE barber demo establish the current website-only direction. The repository does not contain verified client testimonials, performance metrics, or production customer data; future demos must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Sell the business outcome, not a template.
2. Make the conversion path obvious within seconds.
3. Treat Arabic RTL as a first-class web experience.
4. Give each business sector its own visual point of view.
5. Keep demos honest, replaceable, and production-ready in structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Demos must use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible focus, comfortable touch targets, meaningful image alt text, correct RTL/LTR direction, Escape-close interactions for dialogs, and reduced-motion support.
